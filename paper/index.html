Scientific status. This repository proves only structural/asymptotic properties derivable from reversible coupling and RoPE. ASR accuracy, wall-clock speed and measured GPU-memory advantages remain hypotheses until benchmark logs are published.

🔎 What is the claimed gap?

Prior art already contains RoPE-Conformer ASR, reversible residual networks, reversible Transformer blocks, RoPE + reversible blocks in TTS, and even a reversible Conformer for speech-to-speech translation. The remaining research hypothesis is narrower: an ASR-focused RoPE-Conformer using additive-coupling activation reversibility, evaluated explicitly for depth–memory–compute–WER scaling against ordinary and gradient-checkpointed baselines.
